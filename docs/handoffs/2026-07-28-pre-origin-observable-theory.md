# Handoff: Pre-Origin Observable Theory Research

Date: 2026-07-28.

## Goal

Find a new, falsifiable information path for predicting a target repository's
next Task mix before an Origin. Start from first principles and external
research. Do not begin by modifying ALG-012, ALG-013, or ALG-014.

The product runtime remains:

`one repository -> one local Task Pool -> one Selection`

Multiple repositories may supply offline research evidence, but never runtime
candidate Tasks.

## Established Evidence

- Exact hindsight shows that a ten-Task subset can represent future Agent
  responses well. Subset capacity is not the current bottleneck.
- Other Agents' outcomes strongly describe same-Task difficulty.
- Tested semantic transfer, embedding-response transfer, and prequential
  full/recent response composition do not reliably predict the next Task
  cohort.
- No Selector is nominated. Full eligible history remains the primary
  baseline.

Treat these as constraints, not as a complete theory of failure.

## Research Order

1. Read `AGENTS.md` and the product contracts.
2. Before reading prior algorithm implementations, survey external work on
   software-task arrival, repository evolution, temporal distribution shift,
   forecasting, and benchmark selection.
3. Propose at least three materially different mechanisms. For each, specify:
   - the observable available before the target Origin;
   - why it should predict future Task composition;
   - whether an importing user can supply it;
   - how it remains generator-independent;
   - its leakage and failure modes;
   - the smallest decisive test.
4. Then read the prior report and remove mechanisms that merely rename an
   opened-result ablation or a closed family.
5. Select one theory route and define its independent evidence boundary, or
   record that no credible route was found.

## Evidence Boundary

The opened Multi-SWE and SWE-bench Verified outcomes are closed for candidate
invention, parameter search, and nomination replay. A new observable may
advance theory design, but does not authorize another replay on those panels.

Empirical nomination requires at least one of:

- native Task time and historical Result availability with denser local
  Origins;
- an independent complete Agent panel or source family;
- a strict prospective target-repository campaign.

Synthetic checks and outcome-free source-feasibility audits are allowed. Do
not use paid calls, read the sealed holdout, develop a concrete Generator, or
add a trainer, registry, embedding service, or multi-repository Runner.

## Deliverables

- A cited research memo and a decision table for the candidate mechanisms.
- One frozen theory contract and independent empirical test plan, or a
  justified stop decision.
- Updates to the research ledger and `PROCESS.md` only when the research
  direction or reopening boundary changes.

Start from:

- `docs/experiments/2026-07-28-pre-origin-signal-audit.md`
- `docs/research-improvement-backlog.md`

The prior report is evidence, not a golden interpretation.
