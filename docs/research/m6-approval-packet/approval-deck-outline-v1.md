Status: draft approval-deck claim spine, 2026-06-01.

Source of truth: `docs/research/barcarolle-proposal-report-v5.md`.

Deck contract:

- primary artifact: editable PowerPoint deck;
- purpose: help reviewers decide whether to approve Barcarolle as a
  repo-specific benchmark-compiler project;
- evidence standard: current evidence supplies traction and a credible
  validation path, but does not establish formal predictive validity;
- product path: tuning and regression feedback are planned deliverables, not
  already-proven improvement claims;
- user-owned values remain placeholders until supplied by the project owner.
